# RF5.3 release notes
Version **1.7.9 (21)**.

- Retains RF5.2.2 expired-token recovery.
- Retains RF5.2.1 direct BLE/Bonjour/LAN recovery supervisor.
- Cloud intermediate slider frames are limited to 4 Hz.
- BLE and Local Wi-Fi intermediate slider frames remain 10 Hz.
- Final slider value remains durable and semantically ACKed by the ESP.
- RF5 ordering/sequence allocation is unchanged.
